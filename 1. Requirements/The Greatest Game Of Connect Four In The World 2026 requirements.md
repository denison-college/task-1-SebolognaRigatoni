# Brief #
You are to solo develop a console-based Connect Four game. This game must follow traditional Connect Four rules and offer players various difficulty levels to choose from.

# Features #
*The game must feature the following:*
+ Player can choose to play online or locally
+ The game board is displayed clearly, showing the positions of all discs (colour or characters—e.g. ‘X’ and ‘O’)
+ Victory detection: The game must automatically check for four connected discs and declare a winner or a draw
+ Score system: The player starts with 100 points; each turn taken reduces the score by 2 points (for single-player games). Losing to the AI results in 0 points. Winning preserves the remaining points as the final score.
+ Highscores saved between sessions for each difficulty level, recording player’s name and score
+ Collectable special items to unlock something (lore?)
